About Jake Barnes

Knutsford's Trusted Planning Expert

Jake Barnes is a highly experienced planning permission consultant based in Knutsford, Cheshire. With deep knowledge of the Cheshire East planning system, Jake helps clients achieve successful outcomes for residential extensions, new builds, commercial developments, and complex planning applications.

As the director of BCR 1 Ltd, Jake has worked on numerous planning applications across Cheshire East, with particular expertise in areas including Knutsford, Wilmslow, Alderley Edge and the wider Cheshire region.

Whether you're a homeowner looking to extend, a developer seeking planning permission, or a business navigating complex planning regulations — Jake provides clear, practical advice tailored to your specific needs.
